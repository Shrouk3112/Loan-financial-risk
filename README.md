\# 📊 Loan Financial Risk Analysis Dashboard



!\[Dashboard](Dashboard\_Screenshot/Image1.png)



\## 📌 Project Overview



This project analyzes loan application data to uncover the key factors influencing loan approval decisions and financial risk. The solution combines SQL for data preparation, Excel for validation, and Power BI for interactive reporting, enabling stakeholders to monitor lending performance and identify high-risk customer segments.



\---



\## 🎯 Business Problem



Financial institutions receive thousands of loan applications from customers with different financial backgrounds. Identifying the characteristics that influence loan approval and financial risk helps improve lending decisions, reduce default risk, and support data-driven business strategies.

\# 💡 Business Insights



\- Customers with higher education levels achieved significantly higher loan approval rates.

\- Applicants with Poor credit bands received the highest average interest rates.

\- Loan approval rates reached their highest level in 2021 before declining in later years.

\- Homeowners demonstrated better approval rates than renters and other housing groups.

\- Self-employed applicants recorded the highest average annual income.

\- The majority of approved applications belonged to the Medium Risk category.
